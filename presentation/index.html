Our Approach

Hybrid ML + Operations Research

Machine Learning: Demand forecasting, SKU affinity analysis, velocity classification

Operations Research: Slotting optimization, pick-route heuristics, zone balancing

Data-Driven: Real order history, seasonal patterns, growth projections

Real-Time: Adapt to seasonal demand shifts and new order patterns

Our solution combines two complementary approaches. Machine learning predicts demand and identifies which products are bought together. Operations research then solves the assignment problem — where should each product live to minimize total walking distance across all orders? We continuously adapt as seasons change and orders evolve.

Finally, presentation layer: visual dashboard and 3D warehouse for decision makers.

The Science

  • SKU Affinity Clustering: Which products are ordered together?
  • Dynamic Velocity Classification: Fast, medium, slow movers
  • Demand Forecasting: Predict seasonal peaks and trends
  • Pick-Route Heuristics: S-shape, largest gap, optimized for real layouts
  • Zone Balancing: Distribute work across picking zones
  • Adaptive Slotting: Seasonal re-arrangement (garden→Q2, decorations→Q4)
Behind the scenes, we're solving several optimization problems in parallel. We analyze order history to find product pairs that move together. We classify SKUs dynamically based on predicted demand, not just past sales. We use pick-route heuristics proven to reduce walking distance by up to 50% versus naive routing. And we adapt to seasons — spring gardening products move forward in Q2, holiday decorations in Q4.
